
# Anomaly Detection: Theoretical & Practical Approaches

This repository contains our final project paper for COMP 562 (Machine Learning) at McGill University, co-authored by Victor Livernoche, Stephen Mikus, and Haoran Wang.

## 📄 Paper Summary

Anomaly detection is a critical component in domains like cybersecurity, healthcare, fraud detection, and beyond. This paper offers a deep dive into the theoretical foundations and methodologies behind detecting outliers in data, covering:

- Definitions and types of anomalies (point, contextual, clustered)
- Classical algorithms: K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN), Local Outlier Factor (LOF)
- Deep learning methods: Autoencoders (AE, VAE, CAE)
- Synthetic anomaly generation
- Hybrid methods: AEKNN

The paper also includes formal theoretical analysis and mathematical formulations for anomaly detection, as well as discussions on model effectiveness in high-dimensional spaces.
